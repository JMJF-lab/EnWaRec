## 🎯 Project Objectives (University of Oviedo)

The research activities conducted at the **University of Oviedo** under the **Enwarec** framework focus on bridging the gap between chemical thermodynamics and industrial pipeline maintenance through the following core objectives:

1. **Thermodynamic Characterization of Scaling:** 
   Implement high-precision geochemical modeling using PHREEQC to evaluate the behavior of calcium carbonate ($\text{CaCO}_3$) saturation indices and precipitation kinetics across operational thermal gradients ($20^\circ\text{C}$ to $60^\circ\text{C}$).
2. **Spatio-Temporal Simulation of Pipeline Clogging:** 
   Develop robust physical models to simulate the progressive reduction of cross-sectional flow areas, local hydrodynamic shear stresses, and mass diffusion, mapping how mineral scaling leads to severe operational clogging, particularly in pipe bends and elbows.
3. **Deep Learning Predictive Framework:** 
   Design, train, and validate high-performance Multi-Input Multi-Output (MIMO) Stacked LSTM neural networks capable of acting as reliable predictive engines for real-time industrial monitoring and proactive maintenance planning.
